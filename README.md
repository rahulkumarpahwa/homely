Homely is a humanitarian-driven full-stack platform designed to provide support during wars, natural disasters, or housing displacement.
It empowers individuals who have lost their homes to find safe and temporary accommodation while enabling others to offer their homes
or contribute donations to help those in need.
Built with modern technologies — React.js, Tailwind CSS, Redux Toolkit, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and JWT authentication — the
platform ensures a secure, scalable, and responsive experience for both hosts and seekers. Homely goes beyond being just an app; it’s
a community-focused initiative that uses technology to restore hope and dignity when people need it most.
